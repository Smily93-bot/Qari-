Turn any book into a listening session

Open a book — PDF, EPUB, or text file — and Qari reads it aloud, lighting up each sentence as it goes. Tap any sentence for options. Works in Arabic, English, Spanish, French and German.

Your last book and reading position are saved on this device. Scanned books work too: Qari recognizes text on your phone (first time downloads ~15 MB of recognition data). Device voice reads locally. Natural voice sends book passages to Google Gemini to create audio.
