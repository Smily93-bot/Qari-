# Qari · قارئ

Turn any PDF, EPUB or text book into an audiobook, in Arabic, English, Spanish, French and German.

**Open the app:** https://smily93-bot.github.io/Qari-/

- Reads with your phone's voice, or Google's natural Gemini voice (free key from Google AI Studio)
- Library, bookmarks, highlights, sleep timer, recap
- Learning mode: translate sentences, save words, flashcards
- Find books by scanning a cover or barcode; free books open directly
- Offline listening, scan clean-up, Arabic vowel marks (تشكيل)

Everything is saved only on your own device. No server, no account.
